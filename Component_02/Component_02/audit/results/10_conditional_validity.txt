==============================================================================
  B. IS THE QUALITY GATE LABEL-DEPENDENT? (does it remove sick patients?)
==============================================================================
  If SQI is independent of diagnosis, gating is a random filter and
  exchangeability survives. If sick patients have worse signals, the gate
  removes a label-dependent subset and the conformal guarantee is void.
